What this proposes: the note readout and the pitch trace are one instrument, not two cards. The trace is drawn against the lanes of the chosen key and scale, so you see where you are and where you can go. The keyboard echoes the sung key. Key, scale, and sensitivity stay on the performance screen because a singer touches them mid-session; everything else (mic device, pad training, MIDI routing) would live in Setup. What it is not: a redesign of VOXMIDI, and nothing here proves the DSP works. That needs a real microphone.
